Healthy Breakfast Demo

A healthy breakfast is the best way to start your day with energy and positivity. You can enjoy a warm bowl of oatmeal topped with fresh fruits and crunchy nuts, or go for whole grain toast with creamy avocado and a boiled egg. A green smoothie made with spinach, banana, and yogurt is both refreshing and filling, while Greek yogurt with berries and chia seeds adds a protein boost. Traditional options like idli or dosa with sambar are light yet nutritious, and a handful of dry fruits and seeds can keep you energized for hours. A colorful fruit salad with a drizzle of honey makes your meal naturally sweet, and pairing it with green tea or lemon water helps refresh and improve digestion.
